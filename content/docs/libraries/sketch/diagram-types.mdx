sketch draws 33 diagram types: every type that pele draws. This page shows 32 of them. It leaves out `info`, which only shows the version of pele.

Each diagram has three tabs:

- **Picture** is the SVG from `sketch.Render`, drawn by `sketch/raster` in the theme of this site.
- **Text** is the drawing from `sketch/text`, as `tuios mermaid --format text` prints it in an 80-column terminal. It is real text: you can select it and copy it.
- **Source** is the Mermaid source of both.

The tabs on this page work together. Select **Text** on one diagram, and every diagram shows its text drawing. The page keeps your choice.

The pictures, the text drawings and the sources come from the same build of sketch. A script makes them again when sketch changes. Nobody draws them by hand.

## Read a text drawing

- Lines and arrows are grey, boxes are cyan, and notes are yellow. Titles are bold. The data series of a chart take blue, yellow, green, red, cyan and magenta.
- A terminal shows these 16 ANSI colours in its own palette. This page shows them in the palette of the site.
- A drawing wider than 80 columns says so under it. Scroll a drawing sideways when it does not fit the page.

The goldens are the SVG files that pele drew from the 522 sources of its documentation corpus. sketch must write the same file, byte for byte. [Fidelity and tests](/docs/libraries/sketch#fidelity-and-tests) says how.

A Mermaid type that pele does not draw, such as `zenuml`, gives an error with the code `unsupported-diagram`.

## Graphs

Boxes joined by lines. sketch works out the layout in layers, as pele does. `sketch/text` maps that layout to cells.

### Flowchart [#flowchart]

- **Keyword:** `flowchart`, `graph` or `flowchart-elk`, with a direction such as `TB` or `LR`.
- **Goldens:** 114 of 114 match pele.
- **Text:** The layout of the SVG, mapped to cells. When it does not fit, sketch asks for a narrower layout: a flowchart that runs across turns to run down, and columns that hold only gaps close up. Edges that arrive on one side of a node with the same head and stroke join into one arrow. A hexagon and a decision are drawn with slanted sides that join, and every edge on a side meets them at their point.

## Charts

Numbers drawn as shapes. In text, most charts keep their shape: a pie is a disc, a radar and a Venn diagram are braille dots, and a treemap is boxes. The charts are drawn with [plot](/docs/libraries/plot).

### Pie [#pie]

- **Keyword:** `pie`. `showData` shows the values.
- **Goldens:** 2 of 2 match pele.
- **Text:** A disc of half blocks with a legend: each slice's colour, label, percentage and, with `showData`, value. As in pele, a slice under 1% is left out of the disc. Without colour, or in 40 columns or fewer, one bar for each slice.

</SketchType>

### XY chart [#xychart]

- **Keyword:** `xychart` or `xychart-beta`.
- **Goldens:** 8 of 8 match pele.
- **Text:** Bars and lines drawn with [plot](/docs/libraries/plot). Bars stand on the axis line and end at their values, and a tick marks the middle of its row. Categories turn into rows when they do not fit across, with a blank row between them. Point labels and bar values go where they cover nothing.
