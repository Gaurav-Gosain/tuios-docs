`tuios.dev/plot` draws charts as terminal cells. It has eight chart kinds: bar (horizontal and vertical), line, area, sparkline, gauge, histogram, heatmap and pie (a donut or a full disc). It holds no colour and writes no escape sequence. Each cell has a role, and your program picks the colour.

The program maps two roles to ANSI colours, so the chart takes the colours of the terminal's theme.

## How it works

- A chart draws into a grid of the size you give it. The grid is always that size, and each row is exactly that wide.
- Each cell holds one grapheme, its width, a role such as `series1` or `mute`, and a background role for half blocks.
- `g.At(x, y)` gives one cell. `g.String()` gives the text with no colours.
- Your program maps each role to a colour at the colour depth of the terminal. [loom](/docs/libraries/loom) does this with its `Palette`.

## Modes

A mode sets the glyphs a chart can use. Use the best mode that the terminal and its font can show.

| Mode | Lines and areas | Bars, sparklines, gauges |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Braille` | Braille dots, 2 by 4 per cell | Eighth blocks |
| `Blocks` | Eighth blocks | Eighth blocks |
| `ASCII` | `*` and `:` | `#`, `-` and `[ ]` |

## Theme roles

A role names a colour. The names are the TML roles, plus `track` for the empty part of a gauge or a bar, and `heat1` to `heat5`. `plot.SeriesRole(i)` gives the role of series i, and cycles after six.

## How do I

- **Fix the range of a chart?** Set `Min` and `Max`. Both 0 means automatic.
- **Format the values?** Set `Format`, such as `plot.Compact` or your own function.
- **Draw a legend?** Call `DrawLegend(grid, x, y, chart.Legend(), mode)`, and give it a row of its own.
- **Colour one bar?** Set `Roles`, one role per bar.
- **Draw a chart in a TML document?** Use the chart components through [loom](/docs/libraries/loom), such as `<bar data="state.rows" x="pkg" y="secs"/>`.
- **Print a chart with no colour?** Call `grid.String()`.

## Rules

- Every chart is total. NaN and infinite values count as missing. Empty data draws an empty grid.
- Sizes that a caller could pass unchecked are bounded: a grid is at most 4096 cells a side, and a line draws at most 64 series.
- Labels are cut with an ellipsis to fit. Control characters are dropped, and invalid UTF-8 shows as `?`.

## Where it fits

- [loom](/docs/libraries/loom) uses plot to draw the TML chart components, and maps the roles with its palette.
- tuios draws the charts of `tuios tml show` surfaces with it.
- Widths come from `charmbracelet/x/ansi`, the same width functions that tuios uses. The package imports nothing else outside the standard library.

## Versions

The module is at v0. The API can change between minor versions. Its tags have the folder name as a prefix, such as `plot/v0.1.0`. The source is in the [plot folder](https://github.com/Gaurav-Gosain/tuios/tree/main/plot) of the tuios repository, under the MIT licence.
